THE PRODUCT

What the agent does

Reads synthetic stock and usage for store 1618

02

Estimates days of supply from 14 completed usage days

03

Compares validated facts with approved item policy

04

Keeps FDA national shortage context separate from local supply

05

Shows calculation evidence and uncertainty for human review

06

Authorized staff act outside the agent: it never orders or writes

Voici ce que fait l'agent, en six points. Il lit le stock synthétique du magasin 1618. Il estime les jours de stock à partir des 14 derniers jours de consommation. Il compare les faits vérifiés avec la politique du produit. Il garde le contexte national de la FDA séparé du stock local. Il montre les calculs et les incertitudes pour une revue humaine. Et surtout : il ne commande rien et ne modifie rien. C'est le personnel autorisé qui agit, en dehors de l'agent.

BRAHIM · THE TWO READ-ONLY TOOLS

Read, label, never decide

read_store_inventory

Store 1618 only. Any other store is refused before reading.

20 synthetic rows test every rule: 4 critical, 5 low, 7 above threshold, 1 unknown, 3 bad-data rows.

Broken or empty data gives a safe error, never an "all clear."

get_fda_shortage_context

Real public FDA API, exact package NDC, 10-second timeout, one retry.

Every answer is labeled: MATCH, NO_MATCH, PARTIAL or UNAVAILABLE.

NO_MATCH or a timeout never means "no shortage."

CONCLUSION

An agent that reads, explains, and leaves decisions to people

What we built

Two read-only tools, a strict system prompt, five injection controls, and a harness review loop — including one guarded model copy — on synthetic data.

What we learned

Test tools for real. A timeout or "no match" is never "no shortage." Safety rules belong in code, not only in the prompt.

What comes next

Keep improving the demo path people see; use live pharmacy data only after approval. Harness model path is already connected on synthetic data.
